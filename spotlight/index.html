A few things to know

Local Spotlight,
explained.

What does $100 a month include?+

A business listing on this page with your name, category, city, short description, website link, and optional business phone. Your subscription helps support Chris Cerney’s independent reporting.

When does my listing appear?+

Your listing appears once your payment is confirmed. After checkout, this page checks your subscription status. Payment confirmation can take a little time.

How do I cancel?+

Open Manage billing above to cancel through Stripe. Cancellation stops the next renewal. Your listing stays visible through the end of the billing period you have already paid for.

Does a listing include news coverage?+

No. Local Spotlight is paid advertising. Subscribing does not include a news story, interview, video, endorsement, or influence over editorial coverage.
