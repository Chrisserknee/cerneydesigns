CERNEY SELECTS
Video and Event Coverage Agreement
Proposed customer terms • November 22, 2026

This agreement is between Christopher Thomas Cerney, professionally known as Chris Cerney (“Chris”), and the person or business named in the approved booking (“you”). Your booking summary and these terms together describe what you’re buying. Please review both before signing.

1. What’s included
Promotional business videos start at $1,000 and include up to two hours of filming. You can request a business showcase or a hands-on concept with Chris. Event coverage is $1,750 and includes up to three hours of filming on location. Each booking includes one finished video, up to 2 minutes and 59 seconds. On-location filming time is separate from the finished video length. Your summary specifies the concept, deliverables, location, filming time, posting channels, delivery schedule and final price. Any additional expense, such as permits, special equipment or travel outside Monterey County, requires your written approval.

2. Booking and payment
Requesting a date is free. Chris reviews your request personally. If he accepts it, you’ll receive the final scope and price to review and sign electronically. You’ll then receive a Stripe invoice for the full amount. Payment is due seven days after approval or 48 hours before filming starts, whichever comes first. Your booking summary and invoice show the exact deadline. Your date is confirmed once full payment is received by that deadline. If you miss the deadline, check with Chris before paying. Any new date or arrangement needs his approval. Each package is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal.

3. Filming and factual review
You may suggest ideas, people to feature and facts you want included. Please arrange access to the location, make participants available and supply accurate information before filming. Chris may use a production team and selects the shots, script, music, presenter, caption and final edit.

Chris will ordinarily share a near-final cut. You have two business days to send one combined list of small factual corrections, such as a misspelled name, incorrect price or wrong event date. After that window, Chris may publish. Changes to the concept, additional edits and reshoots requested by you are priced and scheduled separately, with your approval.

4. Posting and advertising disclosure
Chris will publish on the channels listed in your booking summary and clearly identify the content as sponsored. Paid event coverage and promotional videos are advertising. Purchasing a video gives you no influence over Chris’s independent reporting. Posts ordinarily stay up indefinitely. Chris manages his channels and may update, archive or remove a post at his discretion, including for legal, editorial or platform reasons. Views, reach, engagement and sales are not guaranteed.

5. Collaboration and ownership
Chris posts the video on the channels listed in your booking summary. You can request a collaboration invite on a supported platform so the same post can also appear on your profile if you accept the invite. Otherwise, the video appears on Chris’s channels. Chris retains ownership of the video and production materials. The booking covers publication through Chris’s accounts. A separate upload, downloadable copy, raw footage, edits, paid advertising, or any other reuse requires separate written permission. You keep ownership of materials you supply.

6. Permissions and claims
You confirm that the facts, offers and advertising claims you supply are accurate and supportable. You’re responsible for permission to film at locations you control and for clearances from people you ask to appear. You give Chris permission to use the materials you supply to produce and publish the agreed video. Chris may omit anything with unclear permission or decline a misleading, unlawful or unsafe request.

If a third party brings a claim arising from materials, claims or instructions you supplied, missing permissions you were responsible for, or your unauthorized use of the video, you’ll cover reasonable resulting losses and legal costs for Chris and his team. Chris will notify you promptly and cooperate. A settlement that admits fault or imposes obligations on Chris requires his written consent.

7. Cancellation, delays and refunds
Contact cerneymedia@gmail.com promptly if you need to cancel or reschedule. If you cancel before filming begins, Chris will refund the full booking fee. If you cancel once filming has started, the fee is nonrefundable except as provided here or required by law. A change of date requires agreement from both sides. Delays in access, materials or permissions may move the filming or posting date.

If illness, severe weather, a venue closure or another event outside either party’s reasonable control disrupts the booking, both sides will work toward a new date or replacement deliverable. If Chris cannot deliver a paid service for reasons outside your control, you and Chris may agree on a replacement or reschedule. Otherwise, Chris will refund the amount paid for the undelivered service. Chris may pause or end work for nonpayment, unsafe conditions, harassment or a serious breach.

8. Other terms and signature
Chris works as an independent contractor. Category exclusivity requires a separate written agreement. To the extent allowed by law, Chris’s liability for a claim arising from the booking is limited to the amount paid for that booking and excludes indirect losses and lost profits. California law governs. Any court proceeding will be brought in a court with jurisdiction over Monterey County, California. If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining terms continue to apply.

The approved booking summary and this agreement replace earlier discussions about this booking. Changes must be agreed in writing by both parties. By typing your legal name, checking the acceptance box and choosing “Accept agreement and continue to payment,” you electronically sign the approved booking summary and these terms. You confirm that you reviewed them and are authorized to accept them for the named client. Full payment by the deadline confirms the booking. Please keep a copy for your records.
