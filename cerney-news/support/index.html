CERNEY NEWS · APP SUPPORT

How can I help?

Cerney News brings Chris Cerney's original reporting, articles and video reports to your phone, with a private Tipline for material you want to share.

Questions, corrections or a problem with the app?

Email Chris at chrisserknee@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or Android version, app version and what happened. For a reporting correction, include the story title or link.

Reading and watching

No account is needed. Pull down on the feed to refresh the latest reporting. Video playback needs an internet connection. If a report will not load, check your connection and try reopening the app. Saved stories are stored on your device.

Sending a tip

Open Tipline, add a photo, video or document, include any useful details and review your sharing choices before sending. You can use the camera or select an existing file. Tips go to Chris for review; they do not appear automatically in public reporting.

Keep your originals. If an upload is interrupted, keep the draft and use the app's retry option when connected. Clearing a draft removes local copies but does not delete material already uploaded. For help with a submission, include its reference if you have one; do not send a private upload link or password.

If you prefer a browser, use the website Tipline.

Camera and photo permissions

Allow camera or microphone access when you choose those features. You can review permissions in your phone's Settings. Existing photos, videos and documents can also be selected through the file picker.

Privacy or deletion requests

Read the Cerney News privacy policy for information about submitted media, metadata, storage and deletion requests. Contact Chris at the email above with a submission reference when available.

The Tipline is not an emergency service and is not monitored continuously. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
