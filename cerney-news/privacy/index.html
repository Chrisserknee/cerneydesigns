CERNEY NEWS · MOBILE APP

Privacy & your information

Updated November 14, 2026. Publisher: Chris Cerney.

You can read Cerney News and send a tip without creating an account. You choose whether to send material to the Tipline.

Reading and saved stories

The app requests published news and media over the internet. Saved stories, search filtering and unfinished tip drafts are handled on your device. The app does not include an advertising-tracking or app analytics SDK. Sponsored reporting, when present, is labeled and does not use advertising tracking. Hosting and service providers may keep technical request and security records, including IP addresses, request times, network details and errors.

Optional app notifications

Notifications are off until you choose to enable them in the app and allow iPhone notifications. A push token, device-level identifier and your selected report/breaking-update preferences are stored privately using Google Cloud/Firebase. Expo and Apple process the token and public report notification text to deliver the updates you select. No account, contact details or precise location are required. Tapping a notification opens the associated report.

You can change your choices or turn notifications off in the app or iPhone Settings. Switching them off in the app removes the active push token from its subscription record. Minimal delivery, preference and abuse-prevention records and provider backups may remain. Push alerts can be delayed; they are not a replacement for official emergency alerts.

Video comments

Commenting is optional. Your display name, comment text and posting time become public only after review. Display names are not verified. Pending comments are visible to the newsroom and to the device that posted them. Reported comments are hidden pending another review. Comments are stored using Google Cloud/Firebase; they are separate from private tips.

Your device stores a private commenting key in iOS Keychain or Android encrypted storage. It lets you delete your own comments and block viewers without a reader account. A browser preview stores this key for that browser session only. Keep access to the device that posted your comments: a reinstallation, browser-session reset or device change can remove access to deletion controls. Display-name and guideline preferences are stored locally. Network addresses are processed for abuse prevention; the comment service keeps a keyed hash for rate limits rather than adding raw addresses to comment records. Hosting providers may retain request logs.

Use comment options to delete a comment, report inappropriate content or block a viewer. Comment settings let you delete all comments posted from the current device. Deletion removes the text and display name from active comment records; minimal operational identifiers, moderation records, rate limits and provider backups may remain. Contact cerneymedia@gmail.com for help removing comments from another device.

What you send to the Tipline

If you submit a tip, we receive the photos, videos or documents you select; video sound; the description, incident location and timing you enter; your sharing permission and name-use choice; and any name or contact information you choose to include. A submission also includes file names, types and sizes, upload times, and technical submission/session identifiers used to deliver files, prevent abuse and recover interrupted uploads.

The native upload gateway uses an IP-derived security identifier to apply upload limits. An upload-session account and tokens authorize access to that submission. These do not require you to register a reader account.

Photos, audio and location

Camera and microphone permissions are used when you take a photo or record a video for a tip. File and photo pickers let you select existing material. The app does not request GPS access or track your location in the background. Original selected media can nevertheless contain precise or approximate location, capture time and device metadata. We do not automatically remove that metadata from submitted files. Review what you select before sending it.

Editorial review and optional identity

Tips are sent privately to Chris for editorial review, verification and possible follow-up. Sending a tip does not publish it in the public news feed. Material may be considered for reporting; name-use permission controls the name you allow us to credit. Leaving out your name and contact details does not guarantee anonymity: your media, writing, metadata or network information may identify you.

Services that process the information

Vercel hosts the website and upload gateway. Google Cloud/Firebase provides upload authorization, private media storage and backend processing. The existing Google Drive and Cerney Tips workflow delivers material to Chris's private newsroom. These services process information needed to operate and secure the service. Submitted contact information is not added to a marketing list by the mobile app, and we do not sell tip data or use it for advertising tracking.

Storage, retention and deletion

Local drafts and attachment copies remain in the app until you remove them. Use Clear draft in Tipline to remove local draft text and file copies. Device backup services may retain app data according to your device settings. Removing a local draft or uninstalling the app does not remove files already sent to the newsroom.

Submitted material and related records are retained for editorial review, reporting, verification, follow-up and operational or security needs. There is no promised automatic deletion date for a submitted tip. Service logs and backups can retain copies according to provider settings. To ask about access, correction or deletion, contact Chris and include the submission reference if available. We consider requests in light of the material's editorial and recordkeeping context; removal from an active record does not immediately erase provider backups.

Security and receipts

The app uses HTTPS and upload authorization. Private submissions are kept separate from published news. No service can promise absolute confidentiality or security. A receipt reports the upload stage shown in the app; it does not mean Chris has read the tip or completed editorial review.

Support, external sites and changes

If you contact support, we receive the information and contact details you send. Do not email passwords or private upload links. Links opened outside the app, including social publications and optional website membership features, are governed by those services' practices. Website memberships and website analytics are described in the website privacy notice; they are separate from the mobile app's no-account reading and local saved stories.

This page will be updated when the app's practices change. The effective date above identifies the current notice.
