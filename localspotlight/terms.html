Listing terms

Updated October 21, 2026

Cerney Selects is a paid business directory operated by Chris Cerney. These terms describe the listing offer and the application and billing process.

The listing offer

The price is $100 USD per month for an approved business profile with its description, category, city, website link, and any optional phone number and logo. This is a paid directory listing. News coverage is not included.

Submit accurate business information and only a logo or other material you are authorized to publish. Approved profile details are public. Contact Chris to request a correction or other listing change; submitted details are locked for review.

Pay first, then review

You pay $100 when you apply. Chris reviews your application within 2 days of payment and emails the decision to your billing email. Payment does not guarantee approval, and your business appears in the directory only after approval.

No further monthly payment is collected while your application awaits review. If you have not heard from us within 2 days, email cerneymedia@gmail.com.

Approval and monthly renewals

If approved, the reviewed profile is published while its subscription remains paid and active. The subscription renews at $100 per month until canceled. Your initial payment is collected on the application date; approval does not start a new paid month. Check your next billing date in Stripe’s billing portal.

An unpaid, ended, or hidden listing is not displayed in the directory. Contact Chris if your listing is missing or needs a correction.

Cancel future renewals

Use Manage your listing to open Stripe’s billing portal and cancel future renewals or update your payment method. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. An approved listing remains eligible to appear through that period while its payment remains valid. Canceling future renewals does not itself issue a refund.

If you are using a different browser, use the billing email sign-in link on that page. If you cannot access billing, email cerneymedia@gmail.com for help. “Forget this device” only removes saved browser access; it does not cancel a subscription.

If the application is declined

We cancel future billing, refund the full initial $100 payment, and email you the reason for the decision. The business is not published. Your bank may take additional time to show the refund after Stripe issues it. Contact Chris with any payment or refund question.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy notice for how application, listing, payment, and browser information is used. For application, listing, billing, or privacy help, contact Chris Cerney at cerneymedia@gmail.com.
