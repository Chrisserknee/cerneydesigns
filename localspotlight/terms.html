Listing terms

Updated November 12, 2026

Cerney Selects is a paid business directory operated by Chris Cerney. These terms describe the listing offer and the application and billing process.

The listing offer

Standard is $100 USD per month; Featured is $250 USD total per month for an approved business profile with its description, category, city, website link, and any optional phone number, logo, and Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok profile links. This is a paid directory listing. Featured includes a highlighted listing and increased opportunities to appear in Chris Cerney’s videos that contain advertising. Specific placements and frequency are not guaranteed, and category exclusivity is not included. Independent news coverage is not included.

Submit accurate business information and only a logo or other material you are authorized to publish. Approved profile details are public. Contact Chris to request a correction or other listing change; submitted details are locked for review.

Pay first, then review

You pay your selected monthly price when you apply. Chris reviews your application within one business week (5 business days) of payment and emails the decision to your billing email. Payment does not guarantee approval, and your business appears in the directory only after approval.

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding weekends, calculated in Pacific time from payment. No further monthly payment is collected while your application awaits review. If you have not heard from us within one business week (5 business days), email cerneymedia@gmail.com.

Approval and monthly renewals

If approved, the reviewed profile is published while its subscription remains paid and active. The subscription renews at your selected monthly price until canceled. Your initial payment is collected on the application date; approval does not start a new paid month. Check your next billing date in Stripe’s billing portal.

An unpaid, ended, or hidden listing is not displayed in the directory. Contact Chris if your listing is missing or needs a correction.

Cancel future renewals

Use Manage your listing to open Stripe’s billing portal and cancel future renewals or update your payment method. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. An approved listing remains eligible to appear through that period while its payment remains valid. Canceling future renewals does not itself issue a refund.

If you are using a different browser, use the billing email sign-in link on that page. If you cannot access billing, email cerneymedia@gmail.com for help. “Forget this device” only removes saved browser access; it does not cancel a subscription.

If the application is declined

We cancel future billing, refund the full initial payment, and email you the reason for the decision. The business is not published. Your bank may take additional time to show the refund after Stripe issues it. Contact Chris with any payment or refund question.

Editorial independence

Approved members are featured in Chris Cerney’s Business Spotlight videos and may also appear in advertising placements in lighter, less urgent news reports. Placement and timing are at Chris’s discretion; no specific video, frequency, or audience size is guaranteed. These are promotional placements, separate from editorial coverage.

Cerney Selects is a paid business directory. Membership does not purchase news coverage, favorable treatment, or influence over Chris Cerney’s independent reporting. Businesses are reviewed before acceptance, but inclusion is not a guarantee of their products or services.

Privacy and contact

Read the privacy notice for how application, listing, payment, and browser information is used. For application, listing, billing, or privacy help, contact Chris Cerney at cerneymedia@gmail.com.
